Each session is forecast from analogs whose outcome was complete by the previous close (no lookahead). The engine takes the top 30 analogs (at least 5 sessions apart) and leans the way their average outcome beat the drift of all eligible sessions. `analog_random` is the control.

- **excess**: average return on signal days in the called direction, minus the market's own drift, in basis points per trade (directional rules), or the extra |move| on flagged days (event rules).

- **q**: p-value after Benjamini–Hochberg correction across every test in this report.
- **eras**: excess in 2012-2020 / 2021-2026.
- **years**: years in which the rule came out ahead / years with signal days.
